# Writing Style

Documents follow the Framework writing rules.

## Script findings

| Finding | Usual fix |
| --- | --- |
| Hard-wrapped prose | Join each paragraph or list item onto one line, and keep the structural line breaks |

## Judgment

Unit: one document, judged against [Writing rules](../../../dev-framework/project/knowledge/dev-framework/writing-rules.md) and, for Knowledge, [Length](../../../dev-framework/project/knowledge/dev-framework/knowledge-documentation.md#length).

A defect here is text a reader can misread: an ambiguity, a clause attached to the wrong noun, or a lost condition, rationale, or link. Concision, lists, and tables are polish.
